Demo enquiry privacy

CoreAML is a product of ZeroDaySecurity and Bex Labs. This form collects information so the product team can respond to your demo enquiry and understand the topics you would like to discuss.

Information collected

Your name, email address, institution, job title, country, selected demo topic, optional message, consent confirmation and the time of submission are stored with a request reference.

Use of your enquiry

The information is used to review and follow up on the demonstration you requested. Submitting this form is not a subscription to a general marketing mailing list. Do not submit customer records, identity numbers, financial account information or credentials.

Storage and access

Requests are stored in the website’s backend database and sent to the product team’s enquiry inbox for follow-up. The form does not expose a public directory of enquiries. Authorized website administrators can access the submissions for follow-up.

Correction or deletion

Keep your confirmation reference. When the team contacts you, you can use that reference to request a correction or deletion of your enquiry.

This is a private launch preview. Before public launch, the product owners must confirm their legal contact details, retention period, applicable privacy notice and the process for handling data requests.
